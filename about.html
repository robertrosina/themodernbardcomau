About

The Modern Bard’s performances bring together folk songs, fantasy ballads and songs of the sea, evoking the warmth and adventure of a rustic tavern of old.

An acoustic artist inspired by medieval history and fantasy, he is classically trained in both guitar and voice, with extensive performance experience across weddings, musical theatre and live venues.

His distinctive repertoire is ideally suited to historic pubs, atmospheric restaurants and venues, wineries, breweries and distilleries, country weddings, and festivals.

  • 50+ song repertoire spanning traditional folk, sea songs, fantasy music and ballads
  • Voice, guitar and ukulele
  • Professional PA supplied and operated by the artist
  • Standard performance: 3 × 50-minute sets over approximately 3 hours
  • Flexible formats for shorter performances, festivals, weddings and special events
  • Performance area of approximately 2 × 2 metres with access to power
  • Outdoor performances require adequate protection from sun and weather
  • Public liability insured up to $2 million
  • Based in Sydney and the Hunter Valley, available across NSW
  • Regular residencies and one-off bookings available
